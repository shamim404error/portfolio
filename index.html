Our Story

Born from a love of understated elegance.

Lumière began in a small atelier in Paris, guided by a belief that true style is never shouted — it is felt. Each piece is considered, each fabric chosen for the way it catches the light at golden hour.

We work with sustainable ateliers and small-batch craftspeople who share our commitment to slow fashion — garments made to outlast seasons and trends.
